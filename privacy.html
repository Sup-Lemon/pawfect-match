Pawfect Match

Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Pawfect Match helps dog owners meet: playdates between dogs, group walks and training dates, and a map of dog-friendly places. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it and what you can ask of us.

Who we are. Pawfect Match is published by Emerge Studio, Switzerland, which is responsible for your data. Questions and requests: hello@emergestudio.ch.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash), or, if you sign in with Google, the name, email address and profile picture Google shares with us.
  • Your phone number, if you choose to verify one.
  • Your profile: display name, photo, short bio and experience level with dogs.

Your dogs

  • Name, breed, size, birth date, weight, energy level, temperament, confidence with other dogs, whether they are neutered, and photos.

Location

  • Your home area, rounded to about 1 km, never your exact address. We use it to show dogs, events and places near you.
  • Your device's location while you use the map or create an event, only if you allow it.
  • Live location, only when you start sharing it with a trusted contact for a meetup. It stops when you end the share or when it expires.

What you do in the app

  • Playdate swipes, matches, favorites and dog groups.
  • Chat messages with the members you match with.
  • Events you organize or join, place reviews, and reports about a place's condition.

Safety

  • Trusted contacts you add (name, phone number, relationship), so they can follow a meetup if you ask them to.
  • Ratings after a meetup, reports about a member, members you block, and safety check-ins.

Your device

  • A push notification token, so we can send you messages, match and event reminder notifications. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

Why we use it

  • To run the service you asked for: your account, matching dogs nearby, chat, events and the map. This is the contract between you and us.
  • To keep members safe: verification, ratings, reports, blocks and the trusted contact features. This is our legitimate interest, and yours.
  • To send the notifications you enabled, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

What other members see

Other members see your display name, photo, bio, your dogs' profiles and photos, and an approximate distance. They never see your email, phone number or exact location. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. A trusted contact sees your live location only through the link you send them, while the share is active.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage, in London, United Kingdom.
  • Expo (650 Industries, United States) delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Google provides Google sign-in, if you use it, and the map on Android.

Some of these providers process data outside your country. They are bound by contracts and safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, dogs and messages: as long as your account exists.
  • Live location: only for the duration of a share.
  • Place condition reports: they expire automatically, between a few hours and 14 days depending on the kind of report.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, as described on the account deletion page.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, receive a copy, restrict or object to some uses, and delete it. Most of your profile can be edited directly in the app. For anything else, email hello@emergestudio.ch; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example the Swiss FDPIC, or the Information Regulator in South Africa.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted per member at the database level, so each member can only read and change what is theirs, and shared items only with the people they are shared with.

Children

Pawfect Match is meant for adults. You must be at least 18 to create an account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above, and tell you in the app when the change matters.
